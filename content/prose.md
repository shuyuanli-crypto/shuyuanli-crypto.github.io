## Lens Strategy

CRM is often treated as a technical function — a stack of tools, a list of automations. Lens Strategy starts from a different premise: CRM is a lens. It's how a brand chooses to see its clients, and in luxury and beauty especially, that choice shows up in every touchpoint — from a single personalized email to the way a client is remembered when they walk into a boutique.

Lens Strategy exists to help brands sharpen that lens — combining hands-on CRM and clienteling execution with the analytical rigor to know what's actually working, and why.

**What that means in practice:** strategy grounded in real campaign and clienteling experience, not theory alone — and a point of view that treats mass personalization and one-to-one relationship-building as one continuous discipline, not two separate departments.
